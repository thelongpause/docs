# Terrain Population Centres
Terrain Population Centres represent inhabited settlements and population concentrations within the World. They are created using the Area Mapping Primitive in the MapMaker. Population Centres include:
- Villages
- Towns
- Cities 
- Capital Cities

## Terrain Population Centres Attributes
Terrain Population Centres utilise similar Attributes to [Terrain Objects](docs/world/TerrainObjects.md), alongside their own unique demographic and attributes, for the purpose of simulating military FINISH. Terrain Population Centres attributes include:

- Height 
- Movement Effect
- Visibility Effect
- Hit Probability Effect
- Health Points

These attributes work the same 

Each Population Centre possesses its own geographic, demographic and economic attributes.
Population Centres are dynamic entities. Their population and physical extent may change over time according to factors including:
	•	Natural population growth;
	•	Migration;
	•	Immigration;
	•	Emigration;
	•	Employment;
	•	Nearby Industry;
	•	Economic development;
	•	Accessibility;
	•	Availability of goods;
	•	Infrastructure;
	•	Housing;
	•	Warfare;
	•	Disease;
	•	Environmental conditions; and
	•	Government policy.
Population Centres may therefore expand, contract, become damaged, be partially evacuated or experience significant demographic change during the simulation.
