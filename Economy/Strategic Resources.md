# Strategic Resources
Strategic Resources are natural resources drawn from a Player's nation’s land, waters, or subterranean deposits, and are used to establish and maintain Industries that have commercial, industrial, aesthetic, cultural, and scientific value for their nation. Strategic Resources can be naturally found within a Nation's borders, depending on the land the Player's Nation holds, occupies or has annexed. 

Strategic Resources are utilized to establish and maintain Industries within the Player's Nation, as well as be traded to foreign nations as Raw Resources. However, Strategic Resource extraction are done by Industries themselves. 

A Nation's Strategic Resources are not infinite and have a set limitation, meaning that over-extraction or poor management will cause them to "dry up" over time. Likewise, antagonist military actions—such as airstrikes or sabotage—can diminish or completely decommission resource extraction, severely impacting a Nation's economy and industrial productivity. 

In a world of competition and conflict, a Player can either capture Strategic Resources from rival nations through conquest or diplomacy, or trade to increase your reserves, ensuring your Nation’s industries continue to thrive.
